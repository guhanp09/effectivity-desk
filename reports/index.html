Work-scope handoff

  1. Engineering: establish current AD/SB/AMOC corpus and effectivity, verify location-specific configuration and obtain authorized scope review.
  2. Planning and supply: confirm due-time basis, labor, parts traceability, quoted costs and line/base window. Recalculate if any input changes.
  3. Maintenance and quality: use approved instructions and authorized signoffs; record actual findings and installed-part history.
  4. Reliability: reconcile subsequent findings and exposure, then review whether the assumed benefit occurred. Close only with reviewed evidence.

These are proposed role assignments and gates, not meetings held or approvals obtained.
